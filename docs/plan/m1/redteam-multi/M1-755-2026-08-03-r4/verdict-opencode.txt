RED-TEAM VERDICT: FINDINGS

FINDINGS:
  - CATEGORY: DOS
    SEVERITY: low
    PROMISE: >
      security.md §Rate limiting (M1-755 amendment): "ONE per-user bucket
      token per invocation that actually makes a translator call (drawn on
      the first cache-miss row...)" and "In group scope the D47 per-group
      LLM sub-bucket is drawn alongside the per-user token (the
      RetryCommandHandler pattern: a group reject refunds the per-user
      token)". "Amplification is therefore bounded at rate-cap x budget".
    GAP: >
      The draw/refund accounting is not precise to the invocation that
      drew. (1) LlmRateCap.refund (LlmRateCap.java:87-95) removes the
      NEWEST timestamp in the user's deque (pollLast), not the timestamp
      this invocation's tryAcquire added. SavedCommandHandler.java:442-447
      (and the byte-identical RetryCommandHandler.java:552-556 pattern the
      spec names) call tryAcquire then refund non-atomically; a concurrent
      same-user LLM-triggering op whose tryAcquire lands between the two
      calls loses ITS timestamp to the refund, so that op's per-user LLM
      cost is silently uncounted — the user's effective per-minute LLM cap
      exceeds the promised 10/min bound. (2) The draw decision probes the
      cache (SavedCommandHandler.java:439) before the pipeline's own
      internal lookup (TranslationPipeline.java:221); the two are not
      atomic. A concurrent same-user render populating the entry between
      the lookups draws and holds a user + group token for a render that
      makes zero translator calls; the reverse race (entry expiring at its
      24h TTL between the two lookups) produces a translator call with no
      draw and no budget slot. These are precision flaws in the accounting
      the "bounded at rate-cap x budget" promise rests on; the bound still
      holds in the common sequential case.
    REPRO: >
      (1) User U drains a group's D47 LLM bucket (5/15min) with ordinary
      chat turns. U then fires a chat message (tryAcquire adds ts1) and a
      group-scope /saved (tryAcquire adds ts2; group draw fails; refund
      pollLast removes ts1 if the chat acquire interleaved). U's chat turn
      is uncounted; repeating the race across minutes extends U's personal
      LLM budget beyond the cap while the operator's per-user cost bound
      silently under-counts. (2) U renders the same /saved page from two
      scopes concurrently; one invocation's probe misses, draws both
      tokens, and the pipeline finds the just-written entry — tokens held
      for zero generative calls (self-inflicted budget drain; in group
      scope, also a group-bucket draw). No cross-user impact in either
      case; both require the user to race their own concurrent operations
      and (for the refund) an exhausted group bucket.
    SUGGESTED-FIX-CLASS: other
