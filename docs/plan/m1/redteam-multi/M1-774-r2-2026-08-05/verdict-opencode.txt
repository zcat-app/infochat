  - CATEGORY: DOS
    SEVERITY: low
    PROMISE: "Per-group reply rate (D47) — a single bucket per `groups` row bounding total outbound replies (fixed or command) within a sliding window. Applies to ALL approval states (pending, approved, rejected) so outbound cost is bounded even for unapproved groups." (docs/spec/security.md §Rate limiting) and step 3.5: "Before sending any reply (fixed or command), check the per-group reply rate bucket. If the bucket is exhausted, silently drop — no reply, no further processing."
    GAP: The per-group reply bucket is drawn exactly once per inbound at step 3.5 (GroupApprovalCheck.check → rateCapBucket.tryAcquireGroupReply, GroupApprovalCheck.java:128). The three new M1-774 drains each emit an additional outbound (the REPLY_CONFIRM_CANCELLED acknowledgement) after that single draw, on top of the rejection reply the path already sent: InboundRouter.java:740-744 (chat body cap), :756-760 (command body cap), :881-889 (probation block). One bucket draw can now cover two adapter sends, so the bucket's "bounding total outbound replies within a sliding window" promise is stretched to ~2× on these paths (sustained ~1.5× once re-arm cost is counted: arm = 1 inbound/1 token/1 reply, over-cap = 1 inbound/1 token/2 replies). The same doubling applies in DM scope (bounded only by the step-1.5 per-user transport cap, not by any reply-level budget), where an over-cap chat body from a confirm-holder now yields two outbounds per inbound instead of one. The amplification is bounded — takeAny removes at most one entry per key and a sender holds at most one confirm — so this is a defense-in-depth reduction of the reply-rate accounting, not an unbounded flood.
    REPRO: A member of an approved group (confirm-arming commands such as /save or /forget are available to any registered user, not only admins) arms a pending confirm in the group, then sends an over-cap chat-mode body. Step 3.5 draws one per-group reply token; the new drain then sends the cancellation acknowledgement AND the error.chat.body_too_large reply — two outbounds for one token, where pre-M1-774 the path spent one. Re-arming between over-cap sends sustains the 2-replies-per-token ratio, letting one member consume the group's shared reply window at up to ~1.5-2× the nominal rate the spec's bucket promises, and inflating the group's outbound adapter-send cost without additional bucket draws.
    SUGGESTED-FIX-CLASS: rate-limit

Step 4.1 writes a group_membership row (and attempts auto-promote, which no-ops for probation users); step 5 then blocks the command with the probation reply plus the new cancellation ack, and the anchor is left intact. An auditor or operator reading the spec text added by this diff is told this rejection "commit[s] to no DB writes," which is false — a per-inbound DB write occurs on exactly this path.
