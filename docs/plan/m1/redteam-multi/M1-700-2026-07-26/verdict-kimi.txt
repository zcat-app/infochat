    GAP: The new --short form replaces per-cluster summarizer prose with
         CategoryRollupGenerator roll-ups (ModelTask.SUMMARIZER), but its
         failure containment is inherited verbatim from the digest's
         optional-prefix path: generateRollupUnconditional catches
         RuntimeException and yields Optional.empty
         (infochat-provider/.../digest/CategoryRollupGenerator.java:146-172),
         and DigestRenderer.renderShortBody then ships the category with

         renderShortBody: `.ifPresent(rollup -> ...)` followed by the
         unconditional footer append). For --short the roll-up is the
         form's WHOLE content, not an optional prefix, so a summarizer
         outage (every category's roll-up failing) delivers a message of
         bare category headers plus steering footers — indistinguishable
         from "nothing to report", with no degraded-form fallback and no
         localized notice. The commands.md amendment in this same diff
         documents the --short gate skip but never amends the failure
         path, so the security.md degraded-form commitment stands
         unmet for this surface. The user is not shown a hung response
         (that half of the promise holds), which is why this is low, not
         higher.
