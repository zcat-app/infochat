OUT-OF-MODEL:
  - The round-2 remediation's own mechanism leaves one narrow shape uncaught,
    and it is worth stating precisely so the fix is not read as broader than
    it is. STAGE1_PLACEHOLDER_SHAPE excludes whitespace from its <id> class
    (DisplayHeadline.java:551-556) — necessarily so, or the space break would
    be re-absorbed into the shape. Consequence: a feed title of
    "REDACTED:AB CD" wraps to "[REDACTED:AB CD]" with no break, and
    "REDACTED:AB CD]" wraps to "[REDACTED:AB CD]]" whose leading occurrence
    IS renderer-synthesized. Not scored as a finding: the spec commits the id
    as hex/base32, so a space-bearing id is not the byte-identical marker
    §Ingest pipeline commits and fails a canonical-regex grep; and the
    perfect forgery (a title carrying "[REDACTED:<base32>]" in full) already
    renders verbatim on main and is the pre-existing class both this ticket
    and round 1's falsification place out of scope. The whitespace variant is
    therefore strictly weaker than an already-accepted residual.

  - The "an unbracketed line always means the reader's own language"
    invariant has now crossed from docs/spec/decisions.md D29 (c) into
    docs/spec/security.md, in both amended §Rate limiting entries — while
    two delivered render sites still emit foreign headlines bare:
    DegradedDigestRenderer.java:78 and SummaryProseGenerator.java:233 both
    still call DisplayHeadline.of(...) with no anchor and no bracket
    (CategoryRollupGenerator.java:329 is a third caller but feeds an LLM
    prompt, not a reader). Round 1 flagged this as out-of-model on the
    grounds that the invariant was NOT a security.md commitment; that ground
    has partly moved under it. Still not scored, for two reasons: the amended
    sentences scope their commitment to the BUDGET-exhaustion path, which is
    delivered, and the residual has no confidentiality, integrity or
    availability consequence — a reader is misled about which language a
    headline is in. Flagged so the user can decide whether the filed
    follow-up (M1-766) is now spec-conformance work rather than polish, and
    whether the invariant should be stated once as a first-class commitment
    rather than twice as a parenthetical.

  - bracketed(), primaryFor() and subordinateFor() are PUBLIC statics on
    DisplayHeadline, while the safety argument the collision break rests on
    ("every operand arrives through flattenToOneLine, whose strip() removes
    leading and trailing whitespace") is a property of today's four callers,
    not a structural one. This is the opposite choice from the same class's
    prepareTranslatedHeadline composite, which is deliberately the only door
    to the private flatten/sanitize primitives precisely so "a caller that
    cannot reach the primitives cannot sequence them wrongly"
    (DisplayHeadline.java:467-479). Not scored — no current caller passes an
    unflattened operand, and the threat model makes no promise about this
    class's encapsulation — but a future caller that brackets a raw feed
    field would silently make the space break forgeable, and nothing in the
    build would say so.
