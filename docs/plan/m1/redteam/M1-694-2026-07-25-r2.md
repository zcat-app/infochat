FINDINGS:
  - CATEGORY: INJECTION
    SEVERITY: medium
    PROMISE: docs/spec/security.md:389-399 (amended by this diff, §LLM output
              sanitizer): "the group-visible echo surfaces (`/saved` reply,
              `/summary`, and the degraded group digest) are additionally
              passed through the closed-list `LlmOutputSanitizer` at
              **render**, where a title or tag whose canonical form is a
              privileged command renders as `[redacted command]`. On
              `/summary` the redaction point depends on the render form
              (`commands.md` §Content): `--full` renders the cluster
              **headline** and sanitizes it there, while the default
              categorized form renders no headline and instead sanitizes the
              whole per-cluster prose — degraded prose included, which is
              where a feed title reaches the reader on that form. Both forms
              therefore redact, and both emit the audit row below; neither
              leaves a command-shaped title unredacted."
    GAP: The final clause is unqualified and is false for the `--full` form
          this diff adds. On `--full` the feed title reaches the reader
          through TWO surfaces, not one, and only the first is sanitized:
          - SummaryCommandHandler.renderBody:479-486 (new in this diff) sends
            `--full` to ClusterBlockRenderer.appendClusterBlock.
          - ClusterBlockRenderer.java:87 sanitizes the headline
            (`first.title()`) — this is the "renders the cluster headline and
            sanitizes it there" half, and it holds.
          - ClusterBlockRenderer.java:112-115 then writes the `summary:` field
            as `cp.degraded() ? cp.prose() : translationPipeline.run(...)`.
            The degraded ternary arm passes `cp.prose()` through VERBATIM, and
            `SummaryProseGenerator.degradedProseFor:193-203` composes that
            string as `p.title() + " — " + p.url() + " (uid " + p.uid() + ")"`
            for EVERY post in the cluster, straight from raw feed bytes.
          So on `--full` a command-shaped feed title is delivered unredacted in
          the `summary:` line while the headline line above it reads
          `[redacted command]` — the reader gets both the redaction marker and,
          two lines down, a working copy-pasteable token. The spec sentence
          asserts a control the code does not perform on that form, which is
          the exact failure mode round 1 flagged for the default form, now
          mirrored onto its sibling.
          The "and both emit the audit row below" half fails in a narrower
          sub-case: the headline sanitize only ever sees `posts.get(0)`, while
          degradedProseFor emits every post in the cluster. In a cross-source-
          linked (multi-post) cluster whose command-shaped post is not first,
          no CLOSED_LIST entry ever matches, so zero `LLM_OUTPUT_SANITIZED`
          rows and zero WARN lines are written and the token still ships.
          Stated without overstatement: the diff does not make the RUNTIME
          worse — pre-diff this was the only /summary form, so `--full` is a
          strictly narrower exposure than what shipped before, and the raw
          title inside degraded prose is the residual round 1 booked to
          M1-691. What the diff changes is the threat model: security.md
          previously scoped the claim to "the `/summary` cluster headline"
          (true then, and still true of `--full`'s headline) and pointed at
          the unsanitized-assembly residual as open; the amendment now denies
          the residual exists on either form. An operator reading the amended
          §LLM output sanitizer has no way to learn that `/summary --full`
          leaves the surface open.
    REPRO: (1) Get a command-shaped title into the victim's world — publish a
