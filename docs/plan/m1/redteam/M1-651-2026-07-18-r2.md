    PROMISE: security.md §LLM output sanitizer, "Match-set derivation": "The

    GAP: CommandCatalogueParityTest.java:104-106 (QUALIFIED_TOKEN_PATTERN) and
         :402-411 (`parseGroupAdminExpectedTiers`) make the presence or absence
         of the literal string " in groups" immediately after a backticked token
         the sole discriminator between `USER_OR_GROUP_ADMIN` and `GROUP_ADMIN`
         (:407-409, `inGroupsQualified ? USER_OR_GROUP_ADMIN_TIER :
         GROUP_ADMIN_TIER`). Those two tiers are NOT equivalent in disclosure
         terms: HelpCommandHandler.java:350 resolves `USER_OR_GROUP_ADMIN` as
         `!caller.group() || caller.groupAdmin() || caller.botAdmin()`, i.e.
         visible to EVERY non-probation user in DM, whereas :349 resolves
         `GROUP_ADMIN` as `caller.group() && (groupAdmin || botAdmin)` — never
         shown in DM at all. So a mis-derivation in the qualified direction
         hands the guard's green light to a DM-wide-visible entry. In the spec
         bullet as written today (commands.md:1313-1317) 6 of 8 entries carry
         "in groups" and 2 do not, so the qualifier reads as the bullet's house
         style rather than as a load-bearing discriminator; nothing in
         commands.md or security.md tells a future spec editor that adding or
         dropping those two words changes an authorization-visibility contract.
         The diff installs no spec-side marker, comment, or amendment recording
         that dependency — the only record is the test's own javadoc at
         :393-400, which the spec editor is not reading. The guard is also the
         sole automated coverage of this axis: LlmOutputSanitizerTest:339-355
         checks name-set equality only (never tiers), and
         HelpCommandHandlerTest:136-144 hand-enumerates `group-timezone`,
         `add-source`, `follow-tag` only and by construction cannot cover a
         command name that does not yet exist.
    REPRO: Not adversary-reachable at runtime; the attack is on the guard's
           soundness as a durable authorization-disclosure contract. (1) A spec
           amendment adds a genuinely GROUP-ONLY command to the group-admin
           bullet of commands.md §Permission model, written in the bullet's
           dominant house style: "`/group-purge` in groups". (Equivalently: an

           the remediation at weakening the enum rather than at the prose. The

           the diff exists to close, and here the guard affirmatively blesses it
           instead of merely missing it.
