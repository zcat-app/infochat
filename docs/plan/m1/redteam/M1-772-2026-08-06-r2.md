  - Availability trade-offs, unchanged from round 1 and accepted by design.
    (a) isMultiLineCommand (:1216-1245) rejects a TRAILING U+0085 / U+2028 /

    trailing LF/CR are trimmed and unaffected, but a client that hard-wraps a
