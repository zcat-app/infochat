OUT-OF-MODEL:
- Multi-instance double-burn of `re_eval_attempts`: `Scheduled.ConcurrentExecution.SKIP` only serializes ticks within a single JVM. If two Collector processes ran concurrently, both could enumerate the same candidate rows (the candidate scan in `enumerateCandidates`, ReEvaluationJob.java:474-500, takes no row lock — no `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`) and each would independently increment `re_eval_attempts`, burning the per-post attempt budget at up to 2x and skewing the infra-failure vs UNKNOWN cap separation the spec relies on (§Re-evaluation job). This is outside the documented threat model: `docs/spec/architecture.md:106` commits v1 to "exactly one Collector and exactly one Provider," enforced by a startup `pg_advisory_lock` (architecture.md:115-116, verification.md:192-195). Given that single-instance guarantee, SKIP is the complete and correct fix; the cross-instance scenario cannot arise in a conformant v1 deployment. Noted only so a future move to a multi-Collector topology is recognized as requiring a row-level lock (e.g. `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`) on the candidate scan, not just the annotation.
