              stated posture is therefore: a bracketed marker that shares a
              rendered field with untrusted publisher text must not be
              pre-craftable by that publisher.
    GAP: This diff introduces a SECOND user-visible bracketed marker with the
         opposite property, and — in the repo's only end-user-facing document —
         teaches users to draw a trust conclusion from it.
         (1) The marker is a per-language CONSTANT, appended inline with one
             space and no nonce or delimiter:
             infochat-provider/src/main/java/app/zcat/infochat/provider/translation/TranslationPipeline.java:341-350
             — `String marker = bundleLoader.get(BundleKeys.REPLY_TRANSLATION_HIT_MARKER, scopeLanguage);

             infochat-provider/src/main/resources/bundles/cs.properties:550

             reaches the reader byte-identical unless it carries a closed-list
             privileged command: `DisplayHeadline.of(...)` at
             ClusterBlockRenderer.java:116, DigestRenderer.java:865,
             SavedCommandHandler.java:413. `LlmOutputSanitizer` redacts

             rule as absolute: "A headline the bot translated carries a
             machine-translation tag at the end of the line — in Czech that is
             `[strojový překlad]` — so you can always tell the bot's rendering
             from the publisher's own words", and "only a tagged one tells you
             the bot rewrote it." The diff also publishes the exact literal to
             forge.
         (4) The positional half of that rule ("at the end of the line") is
             additionally false on two of the three shipped render sites, so a
             reader has no fallback heuristic for spotting a tag in a position
             the bot would not have used: `/saved` interpolates the tagged

             puts the tag at end-of-line. A genuine tag is therefore routinely
             mid-line, exactly where a forged one sits.
