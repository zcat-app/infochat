  - THE WALK IS CORRECT WHERE IT IS EVALUATED. Greedy leftmost `indexOf` with
    a monotonically advancing cursor is optimal for ordered non-overlapping

    algorithmic FALSE NEGATIVE — a miss means no embedding exists. Empty
    tokens from a leading separator are skipped, and `from > 0` correctly
    separates "no words matched" from "zero words to match", so an
    all-invisible original degenerates to no-match rather than vacuously
    swallowing every genuine translation on the post. The false-POSITIVE

    primaryInReaderLanguage then brackets. A publisher can steer a false
    positive with a language-neutral headline, but the outcome is suppressed
    translation plus a correct bracket, which breaches no commitment.
